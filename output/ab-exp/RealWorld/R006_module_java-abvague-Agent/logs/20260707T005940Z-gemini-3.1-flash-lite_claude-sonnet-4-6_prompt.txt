=== Turn 1 ===
Refactor the `Animal` class by moving the breeding-related logic (the `meet`, `breed`, and `giveBirth` methods) into a separate `BreedingHandler` class to reduce the complexity and improve the single responsibility of `Animal`.

=== Turn 2 ===
Extract the food-finding and consumption logic from `CarnivoreAnimal` and `HerbivoreAnimal` into a unified `FeedingHandler` class to consolidate common behavior and simplify the inheritance hierarchy.

=== Turn 3 ===
Introduce a `MovementHandler` class and move the movement logic currently scattered across the `Animal` subclasses into it to centralize the logic for finding free adjacent locations and updating entity positioning.

=== Turn 4 ===
Refactor the `Simulator` class by extracting the ecosystem simulation logic—specifically the iteration over actors and the management of their lifecycles—into a dedicated `ActorManager` class to separate the control of simulation flow from the orchestration of individual actor updates.

=== Turn 5 ===
Extract the logic for counting and monitoring the population into a specialized `PopulationTracker` class, refactoring `FieldStats` to delegate its data collection and validation duties to this new class to improve the modularity of the simulation statistics.

=== Turn 6 ===
Extract the logic for calculating time, day, and state transitions from `SimulatorClock` into a new `TimeHandler` class, and refactor `SimulatorClock` to act as a lightweight wrapper that delegates to `TimeHandler` to clean up the time-tracking domain.

=== Turn 7 ===
Refactor the `Simulator` and `SimulatorView` classes by extracting the user interface initialization and event-handling logic into a new `SimulationController` class, aiming to decouple the simulation engine from the GUI implementation details.

=== Turn 8 ===
Refactor the `Field` class by extracting the grid management logic (the 2D array handling and location-based operations like `clear`, `place`, and `adjacentLocations`) into a dedicated `GridManager` class, allowing `Field` to focus on its role as an ecosystem container rather than a raw array manager.

=== Turn 9 ===
Extract the logic for population initialization from the `PopulationGenerator` class into a new `PopulationInitializer` class, refactoring `PopulationGenerator` to act as a lightweight facade that delegates the creation and placement of animals and plants to the new initializer.

=== Turn 10 ===
Refactor the `AnimalFactory` and its concrete subclasses (`CarnivoreAnimalFactory`, `HerbivoreAnimalFactory`) by introducing a unified `AnimalFactory` that uses a map of functional suppliers to handle instantiation, thereby eliminating the `if-else` chains and improving extensibility when adding new species.
